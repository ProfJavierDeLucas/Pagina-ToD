„Constructing quantum fields from measures on path spaces”

Streszczenie
It is well known that the construction of either a quantum field, or certain probability measure on a path space, are equivalent; however, the formal expression for the probability density functional which would encode the physical requirements of the quantum field is very ill-defined. Technically built upon an asymptotic perturbation theory of Gaussian measures, the physicists have developed renormalization theory as a solution. In this talk, after briefly recalling the reconstruction process of the quantum field from the probability measure, we will explain how renormalization can be better understood mathematically in terms of cylinder measures and their radonification. Then, we will argue that the renormalization problem can, under certain hypothesis, be reduced to that of the corresponding strongly coupled theory, and obtain a family of solutions for the case of scalar bosons whose interaction Lagrangian does not contain derivatives.
