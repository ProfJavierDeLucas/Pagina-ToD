About the seminar

This is the main seminar of the Department of Mathematical Methods in Physics, with the status of a colloquium. Its goal is to enhance scientific interests, in particular those of the younger members of staff, Ph.D. and M.Sci. students. Its motto reads:

That which a young clever and well-prepared mathematical physicist ought to know.

We invite outstanding experts from various fields, mainly those of mathematics and physics (also experimental). Speakers are expected to present — avoiding technical details — results and methods significant for their fields of expertise and potentially inspiring for the young and receptive mind.


Rules

The traditional form of the seminar is a chalkboard lecture, although it is also possible to give an electronic presentation. A natural criterion of choice of the language of the lecture is the paramount requirement of its transparency and accessibility to non-specialists. Lecture notes are welcome and will successively be made publicly available on the webpage Notes.
